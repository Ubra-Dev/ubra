# Support Ubra

Ubra is an MIT-licensed desktop app maintained by
[Oliver Martinez (@oliverbytes)](https://github.com/oliverbytes).
Personal GitHub Sponsors enrollment is pending. There is no live Ubra sponsorship
page advertised by this repository yet.

Sponsorship will support maintainer time, terminal reliability, macOS/Linux/Windows
compatibility, release signing, and documentation. Funding supports the project;
it does not purchase ownership, roadmap votes, a delivery deadline, or guaranteed
support. The MIT core remains freely available.

## Planned monthly tiers

| Tier | Amount (USD) | Acknowledgement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supporter | $5 | Support ongoing maintenance; no public listing by default |
| Backer | $25 | Optional name acknowledgement |
| Organization sponsor | $100 | Optional company name and link acknowledgement |

One-time contributions will also be available. Acknowledgements are opt-in:
contact the maintainer with the display name/link you authorize, or request removal.
No sponsor names or logos will be published without permission. Sponsorship does
not imply endorsement of a sponsor's products.

Once funding begins, the maintainer will publish a brief monthly update describing
work completed, current priorities, and what sponsorship supported. No claims of
charitable status or tax deductibility are made.

## Activate funding (maintainer)

1. Enroll the personal `oliverbytes` account at
   [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors), with Ubra as featured work.
2. Complete GitHub's identity, payout, tax, and 2FA requirements privately. Never
   commit banking details, tax documents, or credentials to this repository.
3. Use the description and tiers above; complete approval and verify the profile
   is public and accepts sponsorships. Follow the
   [personal-account setup guide](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/setting-up-github-sponsors-for-your-personal-account).
4. Copy `.github/FUNDING.yml.example` to `.github/FUNDING.yml`. Its recipient is
   the verified maintainer account, not the repository's organization.
5. Replace the pending text here and in README with a link to
   `https://github.com/sponsors/oliverbytes`. Confirm the repository's Sponsor
   button is visible and opens the live profile.

Investment in a company is separate from sponsorship. Sponsorship gives no equity.
